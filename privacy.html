OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVE 001-A

PRIVACY POLICY (DATA SECURITY MANDATE)

// **Effective Date:** 18 October 2024

1. Intel Gathering Protocols (Information We Collect)

We only collect data strictly necessary for mission success—responding to your inquiries and maintaining operational security. All collection is compartmentalized and classified based on sensitivity.

1.A. Direct Transmission (Information You Directly Provide)

When utilizing the Contact Form (`contact.php`) or direct email channels, the following data is captured:

  • OPERATOR NAME: For formal identification.
  • DIGITAL ID (Email Address): For secure response and follow-up.
  • SUBJECT & MESSAGE: The core mission objective/briefing content.

1.B. Sensor Readings (Usage Data)

Standard network access logs record activity to ensure stability and threat detection. This includes:

  • **Network Coordinates (IP Address):** For geographic and security validation.
  • **Browser/System Specs:** For compatibility and diagnostics.
  • **Access Logs:** Records of pages visited.

2. Data Utilization Mandates

Collected data is deployed solely for the following objectives:

Objective Action/Description
COMMUNICATION To process and issue formal responses to submissions received via the contact form.
OPERATIONAL SECURITY To maintain platform integrity, detect anomalies, and prevent malicious spam activity.
LEGAL COMPLIANCE To adhere to governing laws and enforce the terms and directives of the Headquarters.

3. Retention and Counter-Measures (Security)

3.A. Retention Protocol

Contact transmissions are stored for a maximum period of **one (1) year** for audit and record-keeping, unless an active investigation or legal requirement mandates a longer duration.

3.B. Security Counter-Measures

All email communications are secured via **Secure Socket Layer (SSL)/Transport Layer Security (TLS)** encryption through the PHPMailer and SMTP protocol during transmission. While we employ best practices and security protocols, absolute security across the public network cannot be guaranteed.

4. External Affiliations (Third-Party Services)

This portal includes embedded links and content from external organizations (e.g., social media platforms, Google Maps). These external sites operate under their own independent privacy directives. The Headquarters is not responsible for their data collection practices.

5. Access and Query (Your Rights)

You maintain the right to query, access, correct, or request the deletion of any data the Headquarters holds concerning your identity. Address all such requests through formal contact channels.
